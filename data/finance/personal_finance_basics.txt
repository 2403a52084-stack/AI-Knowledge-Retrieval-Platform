Personal Finance Basics

Personal finance refers to managing income, expenses, savings, and investments.

A budget is a plan that compares expected income with expected expenses.

Saving means setting aside part of income for future needs.

An emergency fund is money kept aside to handle unexpected expenses.

An expense is money spent to purchase goods or services.

Income is money received from sources such as salary, business, or other earnings.

A financial goal is a specific target related to saving, spending, or managing money.

Tracking expenses helps a person understand spending patterns and manage a budget effectively.
