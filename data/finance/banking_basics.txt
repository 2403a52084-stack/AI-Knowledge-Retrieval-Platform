Banking Basics

A bank is a financial institution that accepts deposits from customers and provides financial services.

A savings account is used to save money and usually earns interest on the deposited amount.

A current account is mainly used for frequent business transactions.

A fixed deposit allows a customer to keep money with a bank for a fixed period and earn interest.

A loan is money borrowed from a financial institution that is repaid over an agreed period with applicable interest.

An ATM allows customers to withdraw cash, check account balances, and perform other basic banking operations.

Internet banking allows customers to access banking services through a website or online platform.

Mobile banking allows customers to perform banking activities using a mobile application.
